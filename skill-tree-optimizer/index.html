Tap Titans 2 skill tree optimizer with account import

A TT2 SP optimizer and skill tree builder should not require you to type gigantic damage and gold multipliers. Import your in-game data once, choose your damage and gold sources, then copy the resulting skill tree into Tap Titans 2.

Open the TT2 optimizer

From player data export to a skill tree build

Open Tap Titans 2 → Options → Services → Export. Copy the player data, then paste it into the calculator or choose a JSON file. Clipboard access can be blocked by browsers, so manual pasting is available as a fallback.

The importer reads Skill Points (SP), Max Stage, the current skill tree and available account sections. If your export contains artifacts, enchantments, completed equipment sets, pets, gemstones, Titan Souls research or Transcendence monuments, you do not need to retype long values such as 1e200 into separate fields.

A missing export section remains unknown: the tool does not invent levels, owned sets, enchantments or seasonal bonuses.

Bonuses and systems covered by the account model

  • Skills and builds: six damage sources (Clan Ship, Pet, Shadow Clone, Heavenly Strike, Dagger, Gold Gun) and three gold sources (Fairy, Heart of Gold / HoG, Chesterson).
  • Artifacts and enchantments: primary effects, supported artifact boosters and a separate additive Artifact Damage calculation.
  • Equipment: permanently unlocked set bonuses after selling items, Unique equipment ownership, primary gear effects and selected direct secondary rolls.
  • Pets: Legacy, Exotic and Transcendent pets, passive and active bonuses, equipment pets and supported artifact amplifiers.
  • Mystic Gemstones, Gemstone Research, Necrobear / Titan Souls research and Titan Cards: imported account progression; verified portions enter calculations, others remain diagnostics.
  • Transcendence: seasonal monuments, enchantments and time-dependent effects only when the season and required dates are known.
  • Hero Weapons, Clan Scrolls and active spells: recorded levels, some supported set amplification and spell level caps.

Bring your optimized skill tree back into TT2

After optimization, choose “Copy TT2 skill tree”. In the game, open Skill Tree → Saves → Import, choose the saved build and load it into your tree. You do not have to re-enter the suggested skill levels one by one.

Your artifacts, gemstones, pets and other account bonuses remain managed by the game: this tool does not modify them. If a recommendation involves a different equipped pet or gear item, make that change in TT2 yourself.

Example: Pet/Fairy versus Clan Ship/Fairy

Import the same TT2 account data, then choose Pet damage and Fairy gold. The optimizer calculates a skill tree for that pair and reports which active damage and gold pets and which five equipment pieces belong to the selected setup. Switching to Clan Ship triggers a separate optimization: its skill reductions and eligible equipment are different.

Choose a named pet when you want to keep that pet equipped; choose Automatic for a single owned pet ranked for the chosen damage or gold source. The public mode does not exhaustively compare pet pairs. Copy the resulting skill tree to TT2 through Skill Tree → Saves → Import. Equipping pets and gear remains a separate action in the game.

Only source-supported account effects enter the model; unresolved chance, gold or skill interactions are not given invented values.

Recognized does not mean every interaction is solved

The optimizer currently uses a weighted damage-and-gold score. Some direct account bonuses are numerically modeled; other imported effects are shown for diagnosis until their dependencies, caps and source reductions can be validated. Different damage or gold builds cannot yet be ranked reliably as an automatic all-build recommendation.

Conditional skills, mana uptime, equipment/hero interactions and special seasonal mechanics can change actual results. A theoretically better SP allocation is not a guarantee of more stages in the game. The full-account model is in validation; this is a public-preview guide, not a release certification.

Version and season caution: The skill table was verified against TT2 8.4.0. More recent exports are accepted for inspection but are not automatically certified as equivalent; the app reports their version. Season-14 monuments and Transcendent pets are credited only when the account export supplies supported current-season evidence.

Common questions about TT2 build optimizers

Do I need to enter every artifact, pet, gemstone or monument multiplier by hand?

No. Paste your player data export. Recognized data is imported directly, and unsupported or missing bonuses are identified rather than replaced with guesses.

Can I import my skill tree build directly into Tap Titans 2?

Yes. Copy the optimizer's skill-tree JSON and use the game's Skill Tree → Saves → Import flow. This imports the tree, not your entire account or equipment selection.

Does the optimizer guarantee the strongest TT2 build or automatically equip my pets?

No. The calculation is a qualified damage/gold proxy, not a complete gameplay simulator. You still equip pets and gear in the game, and performance must be checked against actual stage progress.
